# Asset attribution

Nothing in this directory is third-party art any more.

- `brand/` — the Feitor mark, generated by `tools/make-logo.cjs` from the procedural
  portrait recipes in `tools/logo/`. Original work.
- `fonts/` — Inter, JetBrains Mono and Press Start 2P, under the licences in
  `fonts/LICENSE.txt`.

The live world is the Godot project in `godot/casa-da-india`, built entirely from
procedural geometry and shaders — no downloaded images.

The LimeZu **Modern Interiors** tilesets and the Tiled maps built on them were used by
the old Pixi office floor. They were removed together with that floor, so they are no
longer shipped; older commits still contain them under the LimeZu licence.
